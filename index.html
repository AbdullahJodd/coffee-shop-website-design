what's make our coffee special!

What makes our coffee truly special is the uncompromising journey from the farm to your cup. We source only the finest, ethically grown beans from high-altitude regions, ensuring each harvest captures a unique profile of vibrant aromas and deep, complex flavors. By small-batch roasting every week, we preserve the delicate oils and natural sweetness that commercial coffee often loses. Whether it’s the velvety texture of our espresso or the crisp clarity of our pour-overs, every sip reflects our passion for the craft and our commitment to serving you the freshest, most soulful brew possible.

our menu popular menu

Espresso

The "soul" of most coffee drinks. It is a concentrated shot made by forcing hot water through finely-ground beans under high pressure. It has a bold, rich flavor and a signature layer of golden foam on top called the crema.

$8.99

Latte

A smooth and milky favorite. It consists of one or two shots of espresso combined with a generous amount of steamed milk and topped with a very thin layer of foam. It’s perfect for those who prefer a mellow, creamy coffee.

$8.99

Cappuccino

Similar to a latte but with different proportions. It is made of equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and thick, airy milk foam. This creates a light, velvety texture and a stronger espresso taste than a latte.

$8.99

Americano

A great choice for those who want the depth of espresso but the volume of a brewed coffee. It is made by diluting a shot of espresso with hot water, resulting in a drink that is similar in strength to drip coffee but with a distinct espresso aroma.

$8.99

Flat White

Originally from Australia and New Zealand, this drink is made by pouring "microfoam" (steamed milk with very small bubbles) over a shot of espresso or ristretto. It is silkier than a latte and has a higher coffee-to-milk ratio.

$8.99

Macchiato

In its traditional form, a macchiato is a shot of espresso "marked" with just a small dollop of foamed milk. It’s ideal for someone who wants the intensity of an espresso but with a touch of sweetness to take the edge off.

$8.99
